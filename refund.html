MicroTech Cloud LLC · Effective date: July 10, 2026 · Last updated: July 10, 2026

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Not happy for any reason? Email us within 7 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.

Our Commitment

We stand behind SignRight. Every paid download comes with a 7-day, no-questions-asked refund guarantee. If the output isn't what you expected, if something didn't work, or if you simply changed your mind — email us and we'll make it right.

How to Request a Refund

Email support@signright.io within 7 days of your purchase with:

  • Your order / payment reference number (found in your Stripe receipt email)
  • The email address used at checkout
  • A brief description of the issue (optional — but helps us improve)

We process refunds within 1–3 business days. Funds typically appear back on your card within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

What's Covered

  • Any one-time download payment made on signright.io
  • Payments for legal form templates
  • Payments for PDF tool processed downloads

What's Not Covered

  • Refund requests made more than 7 days after purchase
  • Fraudulent chargebacks — we reserve the right to contest chargebacks where a refund was already issued

We do not offer partial refunds. If you purchased multiple items in a single transaction and only one had an issue, contact us — we'll handle it case by case.

Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback before contacting us, we may be unable to issue a refund through our normal process. Please email us first — we resolve disputes quickly and you won't need to go through your bank.

Questions

If you're unsure whether your situation qualifies, just ask. We're a small team and we respond to every email.
