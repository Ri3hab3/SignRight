Malaysia MDAC & Visa-Free Rules for Indians (2026)

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

Indian passport holders can visit Malaysia visa-free for up to 30 days per visit until 31 December 2026 — but must still submit the free MDAC before arrival and pass normal entry checks.

Checklist for Indian travellers

Visa-free ≠ guaranteed entry. Officers still check your ticket, funds and accommodation, and can refuse entry if conditions aren't met. Visa-free status runs until 31 December 2026 — check for extensions before booking travel after that.

Filling the MDAC as an Indian traveller

  • Nationality: India. Passport details exactly as printed.
  • Flight: the flight landing in Malaysia, e.g. MH 191 or 6E 1031.
  • Family: each traveller, including children, needs their own MDAC.

Flying back to India: Air Suvidha

India requires the Air Suvidha 2.0 health declaration from every international arrival, including Indian citizens. File it up to 72 hours before landing.

Frequently asked questions

Do Indians need a visa for Malaysia in 2026?

No, for short visits. Indian passport holders can stay up to 30 days per visit visa-free until 31 December 2026, for tourism, business or social visits — not employment.

Do Indians need the MDAC?

Yes. Indian passport holders must submit the free MDAC within 3 days before arrival.

What documents do Indians need for Malaysia?

A passport valid 6+ months, a return or onward ticket within 30 days, proof of funds, hotel booking and the MDAC.

Do I need Air Suvidha flying back to India?

Yes. Air Suvidha 2.0 is required for all international arrivals to India, including Indian citizens, up to 72 hours before landing.
