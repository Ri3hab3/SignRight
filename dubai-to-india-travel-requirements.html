Dubai to India 2026: Forms You Must Fill Before You Fly

Flying from Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah to India? Passengers who skip these forms are being stopped after landing to fill them on their phones. Here's exactly what you need, based on your passport.

Who this is for: anyone flying to India from Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah or anywhere else in the Gulf, on any passport.

🇮🇳 Indian passport

  1. Air Suvidha health declaration

That's it. No e-Arrival Card needed.

🌍 Foreign passport or OCI

  1. Air Suvidha health declaration
  2. India e-Arrival Card
  3. Valid Indian visa or OCI card

Not sure? Use the free India arrival checker — enter your passport type and landing time and it tells you what to fill and when your window opens.

Both forms are free. Only use official sites ending in .gov.in. Sites charging AED or USD to "process" them are third parties.

Your timeline: Dubai to India

  • 72 hours before landing in IndiaBoth form windows open. You can't submit earlier than this.
  • After online check-inBest moment to fill. You'll have your seat number and good internet. Fill Air Suvidha (and e-Arrival if foreign/OCI).
  • Before leaving for DXB / AUH / SHJSave the PDFs and take screenshots of the QR codes. Airport Wi-Fi is unreliable.
  • At the departure gateSome airlines ask you to confirm the form is done. Keep the registration number handy.
  • After landing in IndiaShow your Air Suvidha number or QR at the APHO / Health Desk, then immigration (e-Arrival QR for foreign nationals), then customs.
Time zones: both forms ask for arrival time in Indian time (IST). The India arrival time on your ticket is already IST — don't add or subtract the UAE's 1.5-hour difference.

Common UAE–India routes

The requirements are the same for every Indian international airport, including Delhi, Mumbai, Kochi, Kozhikode, Thiruvananthapuram, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Lucknow, Jaipur, Amritsar and others. In the Air Suvidha form, select your arrival airport first — the airline and flight lists load only after that.

The same forms apply if you're flying from Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait or Bahrain.

Frequently asked questions

Do Indian workers coming back from UAE need Air Suvidha?

Yes. It applies to all international arrivals, including Indian citizens returning from the UAE and other Gulf countries.

Do I need the e-Arrival Card with an Indian passport?

No. Indian passport holders are exempt. It's for foreign nationals and OCI cardholders.

I'm an OCI cardholder living in Dubai. What do I need?

Air Suvidha, the India e-Arrival Card, your foreign passport and your OCI card. OCI holders are not exempt from the e-Arrival Card.

I'm transiting through Dubai from another country. Which country do I enter as first boarding?

The country where your journey to India started — not Dubai. Select the transit option in Air Suvidha and list every country visited in the last 21 days.

Can I fill it at Dubai airport?

Yes, if you're within 72 hours of landing. But fill it before you leave home — it's faster with a stable connection and no boarding pressure.

Are these forms free?

Yes. Both are free on official Government of India portals.
