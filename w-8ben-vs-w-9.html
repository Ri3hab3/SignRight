W-8BEN vs W-9 vs W-8BEN-E: Which Form Do You Need?

Last verified: 9 October 2026 against the IRS form and instructions (Rev. October 2021) · Not tax advice

Five US tax forms look alike. Pick the wrong one and your payer can't use it. Answer up to four questions below.

Who this is for: anyone a US company has asked for "a W-8 or W-9".

1. Are you a US citizen or US resident (including a green card holder)?
2. Is the payment to you personally, or to a company you own?
3. Is it wages from a job as an employee?
4. Is the income connected with a trade or business you run in the US?

Owner of a single-member US LLC? If you're a foreign individual and your LLC is a disregarded entity, read the line 1 instructions for W-8BEN — the form usually goes in your own name, and hybrid-entity treaty claims use W-8BEN-E. When in doubt, ask a tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between W-8BEN and W-9?

W-9 is for US persons — US citizens and US residents, including green card holders. W-8BEN is for foreign individuals. Each certifies your status to a US payer.

I'm a green card holder living abroad. Which form?

Generally W-9: green card holders are US residents for tax purposes. Check your situation with the official instructions or a tax professional.

My company is outside the US. Do I use W-8BEN?

No. Foreign entities use Form W-8BEN-E. W-8BEN is for individuals only.

When do I use W-8ECI instead?

When the income is effectively connected with a US trade or business.

I'm a foreign employee paid wages in the US. Which form?

Not W-8BEN. Nonresident aliens claiming an exemption from wage withholding use Form 8233 or Form W-4.
