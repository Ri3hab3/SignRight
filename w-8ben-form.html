Form W-8BEN: How to Fill It Line by Line (2026 Guide)

Last verified: 9 October 2026 against the IRS form and instructions (Rev. October 2021) · Not tax advice

Form W-8BEN tells a US payer that you're not a US person, so they can withhold the right amount of US tax from what they pay you — and, if your country has a tax treaty with the US, claim a lower rate. It's one page. Here's what goes on every line.

Who this is for: freelancers, creators, investors and sellers living outside the US who've been asked for a W-8BEN by a US client, platform, broker or bank.

What to keep ready

Part I: Identification of beneficial owner (lines 1–8)

LineWhat to enter
1 · NameYour name as the person who owns the income, e.g. Priya Sharma. A foreign individual who solely owns a disregarded entity (such as a single-member US LLC) should read the line 1 instructions — special rules apply.
2 · Country of citizenshipe.g. India. If you're a US citizen, don't use this form at all, even with another citizenship.
3 · Permanent residence addressYour home address in your country of residence. Not a PO box, a bank's address, or an address used only for mail.
4 · Mailing addressOnly if it's different from line 3. Otherwise leave it blank.
5 · US TIN (SSN or ITIN)Leave blank unless required. It's required in specific cases, e.g. claiming treaty benefits without a foreign tax ID on line 6a, or giving the form to a partnership doing business in the US.
6a · Foreign tax ID (FTIN)The tax ID your country of residence issued you. Required if you're documenting a financial account at a US office of a financial institution, with some exceptions.
6b · "FTIN not legally required"Tick only if your country doesn't legally require you to have a tax ID. Don't tick it and fill 6a.
7 · Reference number(s)Optional. You or the payer can use it — for example, your account number with them.
8 · Date of birthMM-DD-YYYY — month first. 15 April 1990 is 04-15-1990, not 15-04-1990.

Part II: Claim of tax treaty benefits (lines 9–10)

Line 9 is where you name the country you're a tax resident of, if it has an income tax treaty with the US and you want its benefits. Line 10 is only for treaty claims with extra conditions — such as students and researchers, or business profits without a permanent establishment in the US.

These are the lines people most often get wrong. Lines 9 and 10 explained →

Part III: Certification (signing)

  • Signed and dated by the beneficial owner — you.
  • An agent can sign under a valid power of attorney (Form 2848 can be used for this) and should tick the box confirming they have capacity to sign.
  • Electronic signature: allowed only if the payer accepts it. Typing your name alone doesn't count.
  • You're signing under penalties of perjury, and agreeing to give a new form within 30 days if anything becomes incorrect.

After you sign

  • Give it to the payer who asked — upload it to their portal or email it, as they request. Don't send it to the IRS.
  • Note the expiry: the last day of the third calendar year after signing. Signed on 9 October 2026? Valid until 31 December 2029. Expiry calculator →
  • Moved country, changed citizenship, or became a US resident? Give a new form (or a W-9) within 30 days.

Common W-8BEN mistakes

  • Date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY instead of MM-DD-YYYY.
  • A PO box or business address on line 3.
  • Filling line 4 with the same address as line 3.
  • Ticking 6b and entering a tax ID on 6a.
  • Claiming a treaty on line 9 with no tax ID on line 5 or 6a.
  • A company using W-8BEN instead of W-8BEN-E.
  • Typing your name and calling it an e-signature.
  • Mailing it to the IRS instead of giving it to the payer.

Frequently asked questions

What is Form W-8BEN?

An IRS form that a foreign individual gives to a US payer to certify they are not a US person and, if eligible, to claim a reduced rate of US tax withholding under a tax treaty.

Do I send Form W-8BEN to the IRS?

No. The IRS instructions say not to send it to the IRS. Give it to the person or company that asked you for it — your withholding agent or payer.

Who should fill Form W-8BEN?

Foreign individuals who are the beneficial owner of US-source income subject to withholding, when a US payer asks for it. US citizens and residents use Form W-9; foreign companies use Form W-8BEN-E.

How long is a W-8BEN valid?

Generally from the date you sign until the last day of the third calendar year after that. If your details change, you must give a new form within 30 days.

Do I need a US SSN or ITIN for line 5?

Usually not. The instructions require a US TIN in specific cases, such as claiming treaty benefits without a foreign tax ID on line 6a, or giving the form to a partnership doing business in the US.

Can I sign W-8BEN electronically?

Only if the payer allows electronic signatures. The instructions say that simply typing your name into the signature line is not an electronic signature.

Can I use a PO box on line 3?

No. Line 3 must be your permanent residence address — not a PO box, a bank's address, or an address used only for mail.
