SG Arrival Card Official Website vs Fake Sites: Is It Free?

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

The SG Arrival Card is free on ICA's website and the MyICA app. ICA states it doesn't endorse commercial services that charge a fee.

Who this is for: anyone checking they're on the real SGAC site, or who already paid a third party.

How to spot a fake

  • It charges any fee.
  • The address doesn't end in .gov.sg.
  • An "SG Arrival Card" app not published by ICA.
  • Promises of "fast-track" or "approval".

Already paid?

  1. Look for an official ICA confirmation email.
  2. None? Submit free on ICA's site or MyICA.
  3. Ask your bank about a chargeback; keep screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

What is the official SG Arrival Card website?

eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard, run by Singapore's Immigration & Checkpoints Authority. The MyICA app is the other official channel.

Is the SG Arrival Card free?

Yes. Submission on ICA's official platforms is free of charge.

I paid a website for my SG Arrival Card.

Check for an official ICA confirmation email. If you don't have one, submit for free on ICA's website or MyICA, and contact your bank about a chargeback.
