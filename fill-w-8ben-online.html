Frequently asked questions

Is this W-8BEN tool free?

Yes. It's free, needs no account, and runs entirely in your browser.

Do you store my details?

No. Nothing you type is saved or sent to us. Close the tab and it's gone.

Does this tool submit my W-8BEN?

No. You give the completed form to the payer who asked for it. W-8BEN is never sent to the IRS.

Can I sign here?

You sign the form yourself. If your payer accepts electronic signatures, follow their process; typing your name alone isn't an electronic signature under the IRS instructions.

What does the tool check?

Common mistakes: PO box addresses, a US citizenship entry, date format, a tax ID entered with the 'not required' box ticked, and a treaty claim without any tax ID.
