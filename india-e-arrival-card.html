India e-Arrival Card 2026: Who Needs It & How to Fill It

Since 1 April 2026, India no longer hands out paper arrival cards on flights. Every foreign national — including OCI cardholders — must submit the e-Arrival Card online before landing.

Who this is for: foreign passport holders and OCI cardholders flying to India. Indian passport holders can skip this form.

What to keep ready

  • Passport (and OCI card, if you have one)
  • Indian visa details, or OCI card number
  • Flight number, arrival airport and arrival date/time in India (IST)
  • Address where you'll stay in India
  • Email address you can access

How to fill the India e-Arrival Card

  1. Wait until you're within 72 hours of landing in India.
  2. Open indianvisaonline.gov.in/earrival or the official Indian Visa Su-Swagatam app.
  3. Enter passport, visa/OCI, flight and address details exactly as on your documents.
  4. Submit and save the QR code — screenshot it so it works offline.
  5. Show the QR code with your passport (and OCI card) at immigration.
Arrival time: enter it in Indian Standard Time. The India arrival time printed on your ticket is already IST.
Scam warning: many lookalike "e-Arrival Card" websites charge fees for this free form. Only use the .gov.in portal or the official Su-Swagatam app.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs the India e-Arrival Card?

All foreign nationals arriving in India, including OCI cardholders and children on foreign passports. Indian passport holders are exempt.

When should I submit the India e-Arrival Card?

Within 72 hours before your scheduled arrival.

Is the India e-Arrival Card free?

Yes, on the official portal and the Su-Swagatam app.

Is the e-Arrival Card a visa?

No. It replaced the paper disembarkation card. You still need a valid visa or OCI card.

Do OCI cardholders need the e-Arrival Card?

Yes. OCI holders are not exempt.

Is the e-Arrival Card the same as Air Suvidha?

No. Air Suvidha 2.0 is a separate health declaration. Foreign nationals need both.

I'm an NRI with an Indian passport. Do I need it?

No — only Air Suvidha.
