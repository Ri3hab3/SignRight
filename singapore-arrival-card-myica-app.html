SG Arrival Card on the MyICA App: How to Submit (2026)

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

You can submit the SG Arrival Card on ICA's free MyICA app. Since 30 September 2026, the app carries the enhanced SGAC with passport scanning and 19 languages.

Who this is for: travellers who'd rather submit on their phone.

Open the official SG Arrival Card form →eservices.ica.gov.sg · Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) · Free

Keep this page open in another tab if you get stuck on a field.

How to submit on MyICA

  1. Download MyICA from the Apple App Store or Google Play — check the publisher is ICA.
  2. Update the app to the latest version. It's required for the enhanced SGAC.
  3. Scan your passport bio page to fill your details automatically.
  4. Add travel, accommodation and health declaration details, then submit.

What's new from 30 September 2026

  • The enhanced SG Arrival Card is available in MyICA.
  • Passport scanning now works for Singapore citizens, permanent residents and long-term pass holders too.
  • 19 languages, now including Bahasa Indonesia, Arabic and Spanish.
  • The updated app is also needed to generate QR codes for passport-free clearance at Woodlands and Tuas.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MyICA app official?

Yes. MyICA is ICA's official app on the Apple App Store and Google Play. It's free.

Do I have to use the app?

No. ICA's website remains available as an alternative.

What changed on 30 September 2026?

The enhanced SG Arrival Card became available in MyICA. Users must update the app before submitting, and passport scanning was extended to Singapore citizens, permanent residents and long-term pass holders.

What languages does MyICA support?

19 languages, including recently added Bahasa Indonesia, Arabic and Spanish.
