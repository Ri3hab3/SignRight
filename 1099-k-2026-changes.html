Form 1099-K 2026 Changes: Threshold and New Tips Boxes

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

For 2026, a payment app or marketplace issues Form 1099-K only if you have more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions. Payment card payments have no minimum. The form also adds box 1c (cash tips) and 1d (TTOC).

Draft status: the IRS has posted the December 2026 revision of Form 1099-K and its instructions, but says it is still “developing” that revision, and the early-release copy is marked “DRAFT — NOT FOR FILING”. We could not confirm a final release, so box wording or layout could still change.

Does this apply to me?

  • You sell goods or services through payment apps, marketplaces or online platforms.
  • You accept payment cards for a business.
  • You are a payment settlement entity that files Form 1099-K.
  • You received a 1099-K for under $20,000 and are wondering why.

The IRS says the One, Big, Beautiful Bill restored the earlier $20,000 and 200-transaction rule (IRS FAQ announced 17 November 2025). Box 2 (merchant category code) is unchanged.

Points people get wrong

  • Income is taxable at any amount. The IRS says that no matter the amount of reported payments, you report all income on your return.
  • You can still get a form below the threshold. A third party settlement organization may send a 1099-K for lower amounts, and your state may have a lower threshold.
  • Card payments: the IRS FAQ says that if you received $0.01 of payments from a payment card transaction, you should receive a Form 1099-K.
  • Backup withholding: in IR-2026-03 (8 January 2026) Treasury and the IRS proposed regulations under which certain third-party settlement organizations generally do not have to backup withhold unless the payee exceeds $20,000 and 200 transactions. They are proposals, not final.

What to do next

  1. Sellers: track all platform income yourself and report it, whether or not you receive a 1099-K.
  2. Filers: update systems for boxes 1c and 1d and check the final IRS release.
  3. Compare against the 1099-NEC/MISC threshold change, which is separate.
  4. Read the Form 1099-K instructions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 1099-K threshold for 2026?

For third party network transactions, more than $20,000 in aggregate payments and more than 200 transactions. For payment cards there is no threshold.

Did the lower ARPA-era 1099-K threshold take effect?

The IRS says the One, Big, Beautiful Bill retroactively reinstated the threshold in effect before the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021: more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions.

What is box 1c on Form 1099-K?

The total cash tips included in box 1a.

What is box 1d?

The Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (TTOC), up to two codes. Use 000 if any tips were in a non-qualifying occupation.

I sold less than $20,000. Do I owe tax?

The threshold only affects whether a form is issued. You must report taxable income regardless.

Is the form final?

Not confirmed. The IRS describes the December 2026 revision as under development, and an early-release draft is posted.
