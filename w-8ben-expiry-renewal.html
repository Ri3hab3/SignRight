When Does Form W-8BEN Expire? (Validity Calculator)

Last verified: 9 October 2026 against the IRS form and instructions (Rev. October 2021) · Not tax advice

A W-8BEN doesn't last forever. It's generally valid from the date you sign it until the last day of the third calendar year after that year — or until your details change.

Who this is for: anyone whose payer asked them to "renew" or "update" a W-8BEN.

The instructions also say that under certain conditions a W-8BEN stays in effect indefinitely — your payer will tell you if that applies.

When you need a new one early

If anything on the form becomes incorrect, give a new form within 30 days. Common triggers:

  • You move to another country (line 3 changes).
  • You change citizenship (line 2).
  • You become a US citizen or resident — then you need Form W-9 instead.
  • Your treaty eligibility changes (line 9).

How to renew

  1. Fill in a new W-8BEN with your current details — use the free filler.
  2. Sign and date it.
  3. Give it to the payer who asked. Don't send it to the IRS.

Frequently asked questions

How long is Form W-8BEN valid?

Generally from the date you sign it until the last day of the third calendar year after that year. Signed on 9 October 2026, it's valid until 31 December 2029.

Can a W-8BEN stay valid forever?

The instructions say that under certain conditions a W-8BEN remains in effect indefinitely. Your payer will tell you if that applies to you.

What makes my W-8BEN invalid early?

A change in circumstances that makes any information on it incorrect — for example moving country, changing citizenship, or becoming a US resident. You must give a new form within 30 days.

How do I renew my W-8BEN?

Fill in and sign a new form with your current details and give it to the payer, usually when they ask before expiry.

What if my W-8BEN expires?

The payer may not be able to rely on it and may withhold at a higher rate until you give a new one.
