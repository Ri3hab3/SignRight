When Are 1099s Due in 2027? Deadlines for Tax Year 2026

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

Forms 1099-NEC and W-2 are due 1 February 2027 (31 January falls on a Sunday). Most other 1099s are due to recipients by 1 February, and to the IRS by 1 March on paper or 31 March if filed electronically.

Does this apply to me?

  • You paid a contractor $2,000 or more in 2026 (1099-NEC).
  • You issue 1099-MISC, 1099-K, 1099-R or 1098-VLI.
  • You employ people and file W-2s.
  • You use FIRE today and must switch to IRIS before the 19 November 2026 cutoff.

How the dates work

  • The statutory dates are 31 January (NEC, W-2, statements to recipients for most 1099s), 28 February (paper filing of most others) and 31 March (e-filing of most others).
  • When a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or DC legal holiday, the next business day applies. 31 January 2027 and 28 February 2027 are both Sundays. 15 February 2027 is Monday, the federal Washington’s Birthday holiday, so the 15 February date for certain forms moves to 16 February.
  • A leap year does not move 28 February.

The “2027 date” column applies the IRS rule above to the statutory dates in Publication 1099 (2026). Forms 1099-NEC and W-2 have no automatic extension; for most others Form 8809 gives an automatic 30 days if filed by the due date.

Easy-to-miss points

  • Form 1099-NEC goes to the IRS by the same date it goes to the contractor.
  • You must e-file if you file 10 or more information returns of any type in total.
  • IRIS is the only IRS e-file system for these returns. FIRE closes on 19 November 2026.

What to do next

  1. Download the calendar entries for the dates that apply to you.
  2. Apply for an IRIS TCC early if you file yourself.
  3. Collect W-9s from contractors before the year ends.
  4. Confirm dates against IRS.gov/TaxCalendar, which Publication 1099 points to. Our table follows Publication 1099 (2026).

Frequently asked questions

When are 1099-NEC forms due in 2027?

1 February 2027. The statutory date is 31 January, which is a Sunday, so the next business day applies. The same date applies to the IRS and the recipient.

When is the W-2 deadline for 2027?

The IRS general instructions say the due date for filing 2026 Forms W-2 and W-3 with the SSA is 1 February 2027, on paper or electronically.

When are other 1099s due to the IRS?

28 February (1 March 2027) on paper, or 31 March 2027 if filed electronically. Recipient copies are generally due 31 January (1 February 2027).

Can I get an extension?

For most 1099s, an automatic 30-day extension is available with Form 8809 if filed by the due date. Forms W-2 and 1099-NEC have no automatic extension.
