How to Report Cash Tips on W-2 and 1099 in 2026

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

On Form W-2, report total cash tips in box 12 with code TP and list the Treasury Tipped Occupation Code in new box 14b. Contractors’ tips go in Form 1099-NEC boxes 1b and 1c; payment-platform tips go in Form 1099-K boxes 1c and 1d.

Status: the IRS has posted the 2026 Form W-2 and the 2026 General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (issued 29 January 2026). The IRS listed further changes to the 2026 W-2 forms on 19 August 2026, so check the form you actually use. For 1099 forms: the IRS has posted the December 2026 revision of Form 1099-NEC and its instructions, but its own notice says it is still “developing” that revision, and early-release copies are marked “DRAFT — NOT FOR FILING”. We could not confirm a final release, so box numbers, wording or e-file specifications could still change.

Does this apply to me?

  • You are an employer with tipped employees (restaurants, salons, delivery and similar).
  • You pay tipped contractors and file Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC.
  • You are a payment processor or platform that files Form 1099-K.
  • You are a worker who wants to claim the qualified tips deduction and need to know where your tips will show up.

The rules in the IRS instructions

  • Cash tips include voluntary cash or charged tips from customers and tips through tip-sharing arrangements. Mandatory service charges added to the bill are not qualified tips.
  • Enter up to two occupation codes. If tips came from more than two occupations, give any two. If any tips came from a non-qualifying occupation, one code must be 000.
  • Tips are still generally subject to federal income tax withholding, social security and Medicare tax if received tips are $20 or more per month.
  • The employee deduction for qualified tips is up to $25,000 for tax years beginning after 2024 and ending before 2029, for occupations the IRS lists as customarily and regularly receiving tips.
  • Workers can deduct only qualified tips that appear on Form W-2, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC or 1099-K, or that they report on Form 4137.

What to do next

  1. Employers: update payroll so box 12 code TP and box 14b populate for tipped employees.
  2. Look up the TTOC for each job title you employ.
  3. Contractor payers: use 1099-NEC boxes 1b and 1c.
  4. Read the 2026 W-2 instructions on box 12 code TP and box 14b.

Frequently asked questions

What is box 12 code TP on the W-2?

The total amount of cash tips reported to the employer. A box 14b occupation code must also be listed.

What is box 14b?

A new box that reports the Treasury Tipped Occupation Code(s), up to two. Box 14 was split into 14a (Other) and 14b.

Do service charges count?

No. Mandatory service charges added to the bill are not qualified tips.

What does the code 000 mean?

It marks tips from a non-qualifying occupation. If 000 is the only code, the tips are not qualified tips for the deduction.

Are tips still taxed?

Yes for payroll taxes. The deduction affects federal income tax only, up to the limit, for qualifying occupations.

What if my employer didn’t report tips correctly?

Ask for a corrected form (Form W-2c). The IRS 2026 instructions explain how to correct box 14b.
