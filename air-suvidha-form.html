Air Suvidha Form 2026: How to Fill It (Step by Step)

Flying into India on an international flight? You need the Air Suvidha 2.0 health declaration. It's free and takes about 10 minutes. Fill it before you land, or you'll be stopped at the airport to do it there.

Open the official Air Suvidha form → airsuvidha.civilaviation.gov.in · Govt of India · Free

Keep this page open in another tab if you get stuck on a field.

WhoAll international arrivals to India — Indian & foreign nationals

Step-by-step: what to enter in each field

The form has 4 steps and is shown in English and Hindi. Fields marked * are mandatory.

3Health Declaration

FieldWhat to enter
Contact / Exposure HistoryAnswer honestly whether you were in contact with a suspected or confirmed case
Current SymptomsSelect any you have, or None

This is a legal declaration under the Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897 and Disaster Management Act, 2005. Hiding information can attract penalties.

4Verify & Submit

Check every field, accept the declaration, and submit. You'll get a registration number (it starts with letters, like ER2609…) and a PDF with a QR code.

Download the PDF and take a screenshot. Airport Wi-Fi is unreliable — keep it offline on your phone.

At the Indian airport

After landing, show your registration number or QR code at the APHO / Health Desk. If you develop fever, vomiting, rash or bleeding within 21 days of arrival, call 1075.

Forgot to fill it and already landed?

Fill it on your phone right away, before you join the immigration queue. Connect to airport Wi-Fi, open the official link at the top of this page, and keep the checklist above handy. With everything ready it takes about 10 minutes.

Common mistakes that cost time

  • Filling it more than 72 hours before arrival — the portal won't accept it.
  • Choosing the airline before the arrival airport — the lists stay empty.
  • Converting arrival time — your ticket's India arrival time is already IST.
  • Entering an email you can't open on your phone — you need it for the OTP.
  • Name not matching the passport — use passport spelling, not your usual spelling.
  • Not saving the PDF offline — then hunting for Wi-Fi at the health desk.
  • Paying a third-party website — the official form is free.

Frequently asked questions

Is Air Suvidha mandatory in 2026?

Yes. Air Suvidha 2.0 was reactivated in June 2026 for Ebola surveillance. The official portal states it must be filled by all international passengers arriving in India.

When should I fill the Air Suvidha form?

Up to 72 hours before your arrival in India. Best time is right after online check-in.

Is there a fee?

No. It's free on the official portal. Any site charging money is not official.

Can I edit it after submitting?

Yes, until your arrival date and time, using the edit option on the official portal's home page.

What do I show at the airport?

Your registration number or the QR code on your PDF, at the APHO / Health Desk after landing.

Can I use a foreign mobile number?

Yes. The OTP will be sent to your email only. With an Indian number it goes to both SMS and email.

Do Indian citizens need to fill it?

Yes. It applies to Indian and foreign nationals arriving on international flights.

I'm transiting through another country. What do I select?

Under "Your journey to India", choose the transit option instead of "Direct to India", and enter the country where your trip first started as the first country of boarding.

Is the Air Suvidha form the same as the India e-Arrival Card?

No. Air Suvidha is a health declaration. Foreign nationals and OCI cardholders also need the separate India e-Arrival Card for immigration. Indian passport holders don't.
