When Does the IRS FIRE System Shut Down?

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

The IRS FIRE system stops accepting information returns at 3 p.m. Eastern on 19 November 2026. For tax year 2026 returns filed in 2027, you must use the IRS Information Returns Intake System (IRIS) instead.

Does this apply to me?

  • You e-file Forms 1099, 1098, 5498, W-2G or 1042-S by uploading files to FIRE (or through software that does).
  • You hold a FIRE Transmitter Control Code (TCC) — FIRE TCCs start with a number.
  • You file 10 or more information returns in total, so e-filing is required.
  • You are a small business that has never e-filed 1099s and wants the free IRIS portal instead of paper.

Sources differ slightly. Publication 1220 (Rev. 5-2026) says IRIS is the only e-file system, including corrections, after 1 January 2027. The later IRS release IR-2026-99 sets 19 November 2026 as the last day to submit through FIRE. Plan around the earlier date.

Does this affect tax year 2025 returns?

Publication 1220 (Rev. 5-2026) says IRIS will be the only information returns e-file system, “including current year, prior year, or corrections”, after 1 January 2027. If you still have tax year 2025 returns or corrections to send through FIRE, do it before the 19 November cutoff. We could not find an IRS statement on how corrections to returns originally filed in FIRE will be handled after it closes, so check IRS.gov/IRIS.

What to do next

  1. Decide this week: will you file in the free IRIS Taxpayer Portal, through software, or through a service provider?
  2. If you file yourself, complete the IRIS Application for TCC. See the timing calculator.
  3. Finish any tax year 2025 FIRE filings and corrections before 3 p.m. ET on 19 November 2026.
  4. Subscribe to IRIS QuickAlerts, which the IRS recommends for system changes and maintenance notices.

Frequently asked questions

Is the FIRE shutdown date confirmed?

Yes. IRS news release IR-2026-99 (24 August 2026) lists 19 November 2026, 3 p.m. ET as the last day to file information returns through FIRE. Older IRS documents point to the end of December 2026, so always use the earlier date.

What happens if I miss the 19 November cutoff?

FIRE will no longer accept your submissions after that time. You would file the return through IRIS instead. The IRS release states filers will no longer be able to submit through FIRE after the November maintenance window.

Do I need to do anything if my accountant files my 1099s?

Probably not directly, but ask them whether they have moved to IRIS. The IRS release says IRIS has an Application to Application channel for filers using third-party or self-developed software.

Is IRIS free?

The IRIS Taxpayer Portal is free and lets you e-file up to 100 returns at a time by manual entry or CSV upload, according to the IRS. Third-party software is a separate product from the free Portal.

Which forms were filed through FIRE?

The IRS e-file page lists Forms 1097, 1098, 1099, 3921, 3922, 5498, 8027, 8596, 8955-SSA, 1042-S and W-2G as FIRE-eligible. It also says IRIS will accept Forms 8027 and 8955-SSA beginning with filing year 2027.
