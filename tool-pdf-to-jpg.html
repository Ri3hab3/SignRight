FAQ
Common questions
Does this convert all pages of my PDF?

Yes. Every page is converted to a separate JPG image. Multi-page PDFs produce multiple JPG files.

What DPI should I choose?

72 DPI for web or email use. 150 DPI for standard quality. 300 DPI for printing or archiving.

Can I convert just one page?

Yes — use our Split PDF tool first to extract the page you need, then convert.

Will I get one JPG or multiple files?

One JPG per page. For a 5-page PDF you get 5 JPG files.
