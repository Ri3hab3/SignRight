FAQ
Common questions
Is this background remover really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely inside your browser, so your photo never leaves your device. The first run downloads the model (about 40–80 MB), which your browser then caches for faster use next time.

Which image formats can I upload?

JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF and AVIF. Files up to 25 MB work best.

How do I get a transparent background?

Choose the Transparent option and download as PNG. PNG keeps the transparency; JPG cannot, so JPG downloads are filled with your chosen colour (white by default).

Can I replace the background with a solid colour?

Yes. Pick White, Black, one of the preset swatches, or any custom colour with the colour picker, and the preview updates instantly.

What kinds of photos work best?

Photos with a clear subject (people, products, pets, objects) and decent contrast against the background give the cleanest cut-out. Very fine details like loose hair on a similar-coloured background can be less precise.
