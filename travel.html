Travel Forms & Arrival Cards: Free Guides

India and Thailand both require arrival forms to be submitted online before you land. These guides explain each official form in plain English — which one applies to you, what to enter, and when the window opens.

Who this is for: anyone flying into India — Indian citizens, NRIs, OCI cardholders and foreign nationals — and anyone entering Thailand on a foreign passport.

Every form here is free on the official government portal. SignRight explains how to fill them and links you straight to the official site. We never charge for a form, never ask for your passport details, and never submit anything on your behalf.
