Questions
Frequently asked
Do I need to create an account?

No. Upload your file, process it, pay once, and download. No signup, no account, no subscription. If you want your files saved for re-download later, you can create an optional account at checkout.

How much does it cost?

A small one-time fee per download. You only pay when you want to download the result — there is no subscription, no trial that auto-renews, and we don't store your card on file.

Is my file secure and private?

Yes. Where possible, files are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your device. For server-side processing, uploaded files are automatically deleted after your session. We do not store, share, or analyze your file contents.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Email us within 7 days for a full refund. We will not argue, delay, or ask for an explanation.

How is SignRight different from iLovePDF or Smallpdf?

Those tools use free tiers that hit limits fast, then push you into $10–$20/month subscriptions. SignRight doesn't have a subscription. You pay a one-time fee per download — that's it. No monthly bill, no surprise charges, no card stored on file.
