Questions
Frequently asked
How much does it cost to eSign a document?

$1.99 per signed document, paid once. Uploading, signing, placing signatures and previewing are all free — you only pay when you download the clean version. There is no subscription and no card kept on file.

Can I check the document before paying?

Yes. You can download a watermarked preview at no cost to confirm the signature sits exactly where you want it before you pay anything.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. The document is opened, signed and saved entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Is an electronic signature legally binding?

In the United States, electronic signatures carry the same legal weight as handwritten ones for most everyday agreements under the ESIGN Act of 2000 and UETA. A few document types — wills, certain court filings and some property transfers — still require wet ink or notarisation.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no signup step. Upload, sign, pay and download.

How is SignRight different from DocuSign or Smallpdf?

Those services charge a monthly subscription whether you sign one document a year or fifty. SignRight charges once per document with no recurring fee, and your file is processed in your browser rather than uploaded to their servers.
