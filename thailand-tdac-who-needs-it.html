Who Needs the Thailand TDAC? Exemptions, Children & Transit

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with the Thai government

Short answer: every non-Thai national entering Thailand needs a Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) — by air, land or sea, including children and infants. Only a few groups are exempt.

Who this is for: anyone unsure whether they, their kids or their transit itinerary need a TDAC.

Families and groups

One person can submit a group TDAC for up to 10 travellers. Each person still gets their own record, and each needs their own passport details entered. Travel agencies and tour operators are also allowed to submit TDACs on behalf of their clients.

Transit through Bangkok or Phuket

If you're connecting to another country and never pass through Thai immigration, you don't need a TDAC. If you'll collect bags and re-check them, leave the airport, or stay overnight in a city hotel, you're entering Thailand — submit a TDAC.

Not sure? If there's any chance you'll pass immigration (missed connection, self-transfer, separate tickets), submitting a free TDAC costs you nothing and avoids a delay.

Frequently asked questions

Do children and babies need a TDAC?

Yes. Every non-Thai traveller needs one, including infants. Parents can include children in a group submission of up to 10 people.

Do transit passengers need a TDAC?

Only if they leave the airport. Passengers who stay in the international transit area without passing immigration don't need one.

Do I need a TDAC if I live in Thailand on a long-stay visa?

Yes. The rule covers all non-Thai nationals entering Thailand, including visa and permit holders returning from abroad.

Do Thai citizens need a TDAC?

No. The TDAC is only for non-Thai nationals.

Do I need a TDAC crossing by land from Malaysia, Laos or Cambodia?

Yes. It's required for all foreigners entering by air, land or sea. Border Pass holders from neighbouring countries are exempt.

Do diplomatic passport holders need a TDAC?

Yes, if they're non-Thai nationals, according to the official FAQ.
