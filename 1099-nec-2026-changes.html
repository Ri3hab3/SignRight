Form 1099-NEC 2026 Changes: New Boxes Explained

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

The December 2026 Form 1099-NEC adds three boxes under 1a: 1b cash tips, 1c Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (TTOC) and 1d overtime compensation, and the filing threshold rises to $2,000. Use it for payments made in 2026, filed in early 2027.

Draft status: the IRS has posted the December 2026 revision of Form 1099-NEC and its instructions, but its own notice says it is still “developing” that revision, and early-release copies are marked “DRAFT — NOT FOR FILING”. We could not confirm a final release, so box numbers, wording or e-file specifications could still change.

Does this apply to me?

  • You pay independent contractors and file Form 1099-NEC.
  • Your contractors receive tips or paid overtime-style premiums that the law treats as reportable.
  • You use payroll or accounting software that prints 1099-NEC.
  • You received a 1099-NEC and want to know what the new boxes mean.

What goes in the new boxes

  • 1b Cash tips: the total designated as cash tips included in box 1a. This includes tips paid in cash or charged by customers.
  • 1c TTOC: up to two Treasury Tipped Occupation Codes. If any tips came from a non-qualifying occupation, one code must be 000. See what TTOC is.
  • 1d Overtime compensation: the qualified overtime included in box 1a, which is the part above the regular rate, such as the “half” of time-and-a-half. See overtime reporting.

The amounts in 1b and 1d are already included in the amount in 1a; they are not added on top of it.

Other points from the instructions

  • Form 1099-NEC is due to the IRS by 31 January (1 February 2027) on paper or electronically, with no automatic extension.
  • File for any person you backup withheld from, at any amount.
  • Excess golden parachute payments are no longer reported on Form 1099-MISC; report them on Form 1099-NEC box 3.
  • The IRS has a continuous-use revision: the December 2026 form is meant for 2026 and later years until replaced.

What to do next

  1. Check with your payroll or 1099 software that it supports the new boxes.
  2. Gather W-9s and identify contractors who earn tips or overtime.
  3. File by 1 February 2027, via IRIS.
  4. Watch the IRS About Form 1099-NEC page for the final release.

Frequently asked questions

Is Form 1099-NEC changing for 2026?

Yes. The December 2026 revision adds boxes 1b, 1c and 1d, renames box 1 as 1a, separates the address fields and applies a $2,000 threshold.

What is box 1c on Form 1099-NEC?

The Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (TTOC) for the payee’s tipped occupation, up to two codes.

Do I have to fill boxes 1b to 1d for every contractor?

Only where the payee had cash tips or qualified overtime. If there are none, they stay blank.

Is the form final?

Not confirmed. The IRS describes it as being developed, and early-release copies are marked draft. Confirm on IRS.gov before filing.

When do I file it?

1 February 2027 (31 January is a Sunday) for both the IRS and the recipient.
