Who Needs the SG Arrival Card? Residents, Transit & Land Arrivals

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

Unlike most arrival cards, Singapore's applies to residents too: every traveller entering by air or sea submits the SG Arrival Card, with only two main exemptions.

Who this is for: anyone unsure whether they need the SGAC — visitors, residents, transit passengers.

Transit at Changi

If you connect at Changi without clearing immigration, you don't need an SGAC. If you leave the airport, even for a few hours, you're entering Singapore and must submit one.

Land arrivals from Malaysia

Residents returning by land through Woodlands or Tuas are exempt. Visitors crossing by land still need the SGAC. Heading the other way, into Malaysia? You'll need the Malaysia MDAC.

Frequently asked questions

Do Singapore citizens need the SG Arrival Card?

Yes when arriving by air or sea. Not when returning through the Woodlands or Tuas land checkpoints.

Do transit passengers need it?

No, if they stay in the transit area without clearing immigration.

Do permanent residents and work pass holders need it?

Yes when arriving by air or sea, like everyone else.

Do I need the SGAC coming from Malaysia by bus or car?

Visitors do. Singapore residents returning via Woodlands or Tuas are exempt.
