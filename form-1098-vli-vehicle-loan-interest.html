Form 1098-VLI: Vehicle Loan Interest Statement

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

Form 1098-VLI is the new IRS form lenders use to report $600 or more of interest received on a vehicle loan in a calendar year. One form per loan. It supports the new “No Tax on Car Loan Interest” deduction.

Draft status: the IRS has posted Form 1098-VLI (December 2026) and its instructions, and the form still carries a draft marking. IRS Publication 1179 (Rev. 7-2026) said Form 1098-VLI had “not yet been released to the public for filing/furnishing” and that a notice would be posted on release. We did not find that release notice, so treat the form as not final.

Does this apply to me?

  • You are a lender, dealer or servicer that received $600 or more of interest on one loan in the course of a trade or business.
  • You took out a loan after 2024 for a new personal-use vehicle assembled in the US and secured by a first lien.
  • You are a borrower expecting the form in early 2027.
  • You are not the lender: an individual who lends privately outside any business does not file.

What the form reports

BoxContent
1Vehicle loan interest received by lender
2a–2dVehicle year, make, model and VIN
3a / 3bLoan origination date / loan acquisition date
4Outstanding principal
5Refund of overpaid interest
6Original use of the vehicle began with the payer of record
7Final assembly of the vehicle occurred within the United States

Box layout from the early-release draft dated 8/20/26. It could change.

Who and what qualifies

  • Threshold: $600 or more of interest per loan. Each loan is separate, even if one borrower paid more than $600 in total across loans.
  • Loan: a specified passenger vehicle loan is indebtedness incurred after 2024 to buy an applicable passenger vehicle for personal use, secured by a first lien.
  • Vehicle: car, minivan, van, SUV, pickup truck or motorcycle; at least 2 wheels; gross vehicle weight rating under 14,000 lb; final assembly in the US; original use begins with the payer of record.
  • Payer of record: must be an individual, decedent’s estate or nongrantor trust. Do not file if the payer is a nonresident alien.
  • Related lenders: loans owed to a related person are not specified passenger vehicle loans.

Borrowers: the draft 2026 Schedule 1-A has a Part IV, “No Tax on Car Loan Interest”, and notes that certain vehicle loan interest paid in 2026 should be reported on Form 1098-VLI box 1. We do not state the deduction amount or income limits here because we did not verify them.

What to do next

  1. Lenders: confirm your loan servicing system can flag first-lien vehicle loans originated after 2024 and capture VIN and final-assembly data.
  2. Check the IRS page for the final release before filing.
  3. Borrowers: keep your lender statements and VIN, and wait for the form in early 2027.
  4. Read the Form 1098-VLI instructions.

Frequently asked questions

What is Form 1098-VLI?

A Vehicle Loan Interest Statement that a business lender files to report $600 or more of specified passenger vehicle loan interest received from an individual, estate or nongrantor trust.

Do I get a form for each loan?

Yes. The $600 threshold applies loan by loan.

Does a lender file for a used car loan?

The instructions require that original use of the vehicle begin with the payer of record, so a used vehicle generally does not qualify.

Is the form final?

Not confirmed. Publication 1179 (Rev. 7-2026) listed it among forms not yet released for filing and furnishing.

Does a loan before 2025 qualify?

No. A specified passenger vehicle loan is debt incurred after 2024.

When will borrowers get it?

Statements are due to borrowers on 31 January, which becomes 1 February 2027.
