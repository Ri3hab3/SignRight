How to File a 1099 Through IRIS

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

Get an IRIS TCC, sign in to the IRIS Taxpayer Portal, then type each form in or upload a CSV. The Portal is free and the IRS says it lets you e-file up to 100 returns at a time.

Does this apply to me?

  • You paid contractors, landlords or others and must file Forms 1099.
  • You file only a few returns and prefer a free web tool over paid software.
  • You used FIRE before and need to switch.
  • You file 10 or more returns in total, so you must e-file.

Filing in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal

  1. Get an IRIS TCC. You need one to use the Portal. See the TCC guide.
  2. Choose entry or upload. Enter each form by hand, or upload a CSV using the template for that form (found on the IRIS Dashboard under “Upload CSV with Form Data”).
  3. Respect CSV rules. Each CSV holds up to 100 records (not counting the header), one form type and one tax year per file. Multiple payers are allowed. You can submit unlimited files.
  4. Submit. You do not file a Form 1096 with IRIS; its data is in the submission header.
  5. Furnish recipient copies. Download and print them from the Portal’s Submitted Forms tile, then send by the recipient deadline.

Sources: IRS IRIS FAQs and Publication 5717 (Taxpayer Portal user guide).

Using software instead (A2A)

The Application to Application (A2A) channel is for third-party or self-developed software and larger volumes. A2A uses XML, with a limit of 100MB per file. The IRS runs an Assurance Testing System (ATS) for A2A testing; as of 7 October 2026, ATS transmissions use SchemaVersionNum 5.0.7 for tax years 2022 through 2026.

What to do next

  1. Apply for your IRIS TCC now.
  2. Download the CSV template for your form inside the Portal, and follow the Publication 5717 guide.
  3. Check your deadlines and calendar them.
  4. Collect payee names and TINs early. Contractors can give you Form W-9.

Frequently asked questions

How do I file a 1099 through IRIS?

Apply for an IRIS TCC, sign in to the Taxpayer Portal, then enter forms manually or upload CSV files. The Portal is free.

Is the IRIS portal free?

Yes. The IRS describes the IRIS Taxpayer Portal as a free platform.

How many 1099s can I file at once?

Each CSV file holds up to 100 records, and the IRS says the Portal lets you e-file up to 100 returns at a time. You can submit more than one file.

Do I send Form 1096?

No. The IRS says no separate transmittal such as Form 1096 is needed through IRIS.

Can I mix 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC in one CSV?

No. Each form type uses its own template, and all rows in a file must be the same form type and tax year.

Do I still send payees a copy?

Yes. You must furnish statements to recipients. The Portal lets you download recipient copies.
