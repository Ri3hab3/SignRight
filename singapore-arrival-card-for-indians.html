Singapore Entry Rules for Indians: Visa + SG Arrival Card (2026)

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

Indian travellers to Singapore usually need two separate things: a Singapore visa before travel, and the free SG Arrival Card within 3 days before arrival.

Who this is for: Indian passport holders visiting Singapore.

Checklist for Indian travellers

Check visa rules on ICA's site. Visa requirements and any transit exemptions depend on your travel plans. Confirm on ica.gov.sg before booking.

Filling the SGAC as an Indian traveller

  • Use the MyICA app to scan your passport and avoid typos.
  • Enter the flight landing in Singapore, e.g. SQ 403 or AI 2380.
  • Copy your hotel address from your booking.
  • Answer the health declaration honestly.

Frequently asked questions

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore?

Generally yes. Indian passport holders usually need a Singapore visa before travelling, applied through an ICA-authorised channel. Check ICA for current rules and any transit exemptions.

Do Indians need the SG Arrival Card?

Yes. All visitors arriving by air or sea must submit the free SGAC within 3 days before arrival.

Is the SG Arrival Card the same as a Singapore visa?

No. The SGAC is an arrival registration and health declaration. A visa is a separate permission to enter.

Do I need Air Suvidha flying back to India?

Yes. Air Suvidha 2.0 is required for all international arrivals to India, up to 72 hours before landing.
