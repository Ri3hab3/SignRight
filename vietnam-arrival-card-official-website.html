Vietnam Arrival Card Official Website vs Fake Sites

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

Vietnam's digital arrival declaration is free at prearrival.immigration.gov.vn. Any site charging to submit it is a reseller.

How to spot a fake

  • It charges any fee for the declaration.
  • The address doesn't end in .gov.vn.
  • It's a sponsored ad — type the address yourself.
  • It bundles the declaration with a marked-up e-visa.

Already paid?

  1. Check you received a valid QR code.
  2. None? Submit free on the official portal.
  3. Ask your bank about a chargeback; keep screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

What is the official website for Vietnam's arrival declaration?

prearrival.immigration.gov.vn. Vietnamese government sites end in .gov.vn.

Is it free?

Yes. Sites charging a fee are third-party resellers.

I paid a site. What now?

Check that you have a valid QR code. If not, submit free on the official portal and ask your bank about a chargeback.
