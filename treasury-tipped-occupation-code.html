What Is the Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (TTOC)?

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

A TTOC is the three-digit code the IRS assigns to each occupation that customarily and regularly receives tips. Payers report it next to cash tips on 2026 Forms W-2, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC and 1099-K.

Does this apply to me?

  • You are an employer or payer that reports cash tips.
  • You are a tipped worker who wants the qualified tips deduction.
  • You see a code in box 14b (W-2), 1c (1099-NEC) or 1d (1099-K) and wonder what it means.
  • You work two jobs, only one of them tipped.

How TTOC works

  • The IRS list of occupations is classified by the Treasury Tipped Occupation Code system. Each occupation has a three-digit code and description.
  • The final regulations (IR-2026-49, 10 April 2026) list more than 70 tipped occupations in eight categories, from the 100s (beverage and food service) to the 800s (transportation and delivery).
  • Only occupations that customarily and regularly received tips on or before 31 December 2024 are included.
  • Tips from an occupation not on the list are not eligible for the deduction.

Where the TTOC goes

FormBox
W-2Box 14b (up to two codes), alongside box 12 code TP
1099-NECBox 1c
1099-MISCBox 13b
1099-KBox 1d

Enter up to two codes. If tips came from more than two occupations, enter any two. If any tips came from a non-qualifying occupation, one code must be 000. If 000 is the only code, the tips are not qualified tips.

Where to find your code

The IRS instructions point to IRS.gov/TippedOccupations and IRS.gov/TTOC for the list of codes. The list itself is in the final regulations (Federal Register). We do not reproduce individual codes here because we could not verify the final numbers on a page we fetched, and wrong codes cause wrong forms.

What to do next

  1. Employers: find each tipped job title on the IRS list and store its code in payroll.
  2. Workers: check your W-2 box 14b matches your real job.
  3. Read how to report cash tips.
  4. Read the IRS release on the final list.

Frequently asked questions

What does TTOC stand for?

Treasury Tipped Occupation Code.

How many digits is a TTOC?

Three. Each occupation has a three-digit code and a description.

What does 000 mean?

It marks tips from a non-qualifying occupation. If it is the only code, the tips are not qualified tips for the deduction.

Can I have more than one TTOC?

Yes, up to two on the form. If you had tips in more than two occupations, the payer lists any two.

Are gig workers covered?

The IRS says gig workers and other self-employed people can qualify if their occupation is on the list. The deduction for self-employed people is limited to net income.

Which occupations are on the list?

More than 70, in eight categories. See the IRS-linked final regulations for the exact list.
