FAQ
Common questions
What is PDF/A?

An ISO standard (ISO 19005) for long-term digital archiving. All fonts embedded, no external dependencies.

When do I need PDF/A?

Court filings, government records, medical records, financial archives, and compliance documents.

Difference between PDF/A-1b and PDF/A-2b?

PDF/A-1b is older, more compatible. PDF/A-2b supports JPEG 2000 and transparency. PDF/A-2b recommended.

Will my PDF look different?

Rarely. Some transparency effects may be flattened. Text and images are preserved.
