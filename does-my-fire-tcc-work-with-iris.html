Does My FIRE TCC Work With IRIS?

Last verified: 31 October 2026 against IRS.gov sources listed below · General information, not tax advice. Confirm with the IRS or a qualified tax professional before you act.

No. The IRS tells current FIRE users to complete a separate IRIS Application for TCC. An IRIS TCC starts with the letter D and works only in IRIS, while FIRE TCCs start with a number.

Does this apply to me?

  • You hold a FIRE TCC and upload your own files to FIRE.
  • You are not sure whether your software vendor or accountant holds the TCC.
  • You plan to e-file Forms 1099 in early 2027.
  • You are an agency, transmitter or software developer.

What the IRS says

  • Publication 1220 (Rev. 5-2026): “Current FIRE users must complete an Information Returns Intake System (IRIS) Application for TCC and transition to IRIS for electronic filing beginning with the 2027 filing season.”
  • IRS e-file pages: FIRE TCCs start with a numeric character; IRIS TCCs start with “D”; and an IRIS TCC “can only be used for IRIS”.
  • IR-2026-99 asks filers to “complete an IRIS Application for TCC”.

We found no IRS statement that FIRE TCCs are converted automatically, so treat a new IRIS application as required.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new TCC for IRIS?

If you file your own returns, yes. The IRS requires current FIRE users to complete an IRIS Application for TCC.

Can I keep using my FIRE TCC after 19 November 2026?

FIRE stops accepting submissions after 3 p.m. ET on that date, so there is nothing left to use it for. Pub. 1220 also says existing FIRE applications become read-only after December 2026.

My payroll provider files my 1099s. Do I need a TCC?

Usually the provider needs one, not you. Ask them whether they will submit through IRIS in 2027.

How do I know if a TCC is an IRIS TCC?

IRIS TCCs start with the letter D, and FIRE TCCs start with a number, according to the IRS e-file page.

Where can I check my application?

Sign in through the IRS IRIS Application for TCC page. The Application Summary page shows your TCC once status is Completed.
