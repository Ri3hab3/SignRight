Vietnam for Indians: e-Visa + Digital Arrival Card (2026)

Last verified: 3 October 2026 · Independent guide · Not affiliated with any government

Indian travellers to Vietnam need two separate things: an e-visa (applied for in advance) and the free pre-arrival declaration (within 3 days of landing).

Frequently asked questions

Do Indians need a visa for Vietnam?

Yes. Indian passport holders need a visa. Vietnam's official e-visa allows stays up to 90 days, single or multiple entry, applied for at evisa.gov.vn.

How much is the Vietnam e-visa?

On the official portal, USD 25 for single entry and USD 50 for multiple entry. Check the portal for current fees.

Do Indians need the digital arrival declaration too?

Yes. It's separate from the visa. Submit it free up to 3 days before arrival.

Do I need Air Suvidha flying back to India?

Yes. Air Suvidha 2.0 is required for all international arrivals to India, up to 72 hours before landing.
